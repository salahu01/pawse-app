## Momo (kid)

All Momo voice lines (kid_ask_0..2, kid_happy, kid_goal, kid_sad, kid_plead, kid_break, kid_habit, kid_breakstart, kid_breakdone) were generated
locally with Kokoro TTS (Apache-2.0), voice `af_bella`, pitched up for a childlike voice.
Giggle in kid_happy / kid_goal: [Four Years Old child laughing.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Four_Years_Old_child_laughing.ogg) by morgantj — CC BY 3.0.
