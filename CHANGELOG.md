# Changelog

All notable changes to Pawse are documented here. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-05

First public release.

### Added
- Five 3D pets that walk onto your screen and ask: Momo the Chibi, Mochi the Cat, Pip the Penguin,
  Yuzu the Capybara and Boo the Ghost Bunny. Drop any `.usdz` model in to add your own.
- Water tracker with daily goal, glass size, streaks, a 7-day chart and a month calendar.
- Custom habits with any reminder interval (stretch every 30 min, walk every 45 min, …), streaks and history.
- Screen-time tracking from keyboard/mouse activity, with automatic pause when you step away.
- Break reminders every N minutes of continuous use, and a calm full-screen break countdown with tips.
- Hard block: for water, any habit or breaks, the screen stays covered until you say yes
  (emergency exit: hold Esc for 5 seconds).
- Voices and sounds: Momo's voice lines with curious, happy and sad expressions, real animal recordings.
- Launch at login, active hours, pause, volume, custom sounds folder.
